Project intake

Tell us the outcome you need.

Share a few details to send a structured project brief through our configured intake service. If the service cannot confirm receipt, the page will explain the fallback and help you keep a local copy.

What helps us scope well?

One goal, an example or reference, must-have deliverables, deadline, budget range, and any access or privacy constraints.

When online intake is active, Send project enquiry submits your brief and returns a reference. If the service is unavailable, use the Instagram button to copy your brief and send it manually. Never include passwords, API keys, or sensitive customer data.

Submitting sends the brief only to the configured Nexora AI intake service. If that service is not configured, a local copy is downloaded and no enquiry is sent automatically.
