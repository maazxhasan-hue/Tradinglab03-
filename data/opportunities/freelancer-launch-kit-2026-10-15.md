# Freelancer Launch Kit — 2026-10-15

Goal: pursue suitable web/automation/content projects without paying Codeur's required subscription upfront.

## Recommended immediate path

- **Do not pay for Codeur yet.** Its official pricing page says provider accounts can be created free, but sending offers requires a subscription from €31.90/month before tax, plus a 4% winning-project commission with a €5 minimum before tax. Source: https://www.codeur.com/pages/tarifs-codeur-com
- **Try Operalyn as a no-subscription option to evaluate:** https://www.operalyn.com/ . Its current homepage advertises no setup fee, no monthly subscription, and 0% commission, but verify its actual terms, client activity, payout method, and legitimacy before relying on it. It is a third-party platform and has not been independently audited here.
- **Upwork is a secondary option, not guaranteed free bidding:** https://www.upwork.com/ . Basic membership is free, but proposals use Connects; the official page says some freelancers may receive 10 free Connects per month, and the amount needed varies by job. Avoid buying Connects until you choose a viable project. Source: https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free
- No external account was created, no personal information was submitted, no money was spent, and no proposals were sent. Account creation requires the account holder's identity, consent, and any required verification.

## Profile draft (use only if accurate)

**Headline:** Website & Digital Workflow Support | Landing Pages, WordPress Updates, Content Operations

**Short bio:**
I help small businesses organize their online presence through clear website pages, landing-page updates, content workflows, and practical digital improvements. I start by confirming the goal, scope, assets, deadline, and acceptance criteria, then break the work into reviewable milestones. I communicate clearly and test the agreed deliverables before handoff.

I take on only work that matches my actual skills and available time. I do not promise search rankings, ad performance, sales, or other outcomes outside my control.

**Important:** This is a positioning draft, not proof of past client work. Add only skills you can personally deliver. Do not claim WordPress, coding, SEO, paid ads, or portfolio experience unless you can demonstrate it.

## Proposal template — English

Hello,

I read your brief and understand that you need [specific deliverable] for [business goal]. Before confirming a fixed price and deadline, I would clarify [one or two scope questions].

My proposed approach is:
1. Confirm the deliverables, assets, and acceptance criteria.
2. Share a first version for review.
3. Apply the agreed revisions and test the final result.
4. Hand over the files and a short usage note.

The price and timeline should be agreed after the scope is clear. I will only commit to features I can implement and verify.

Would you be able to share [most important missing asset/detail]?

Regards

## Proposal template — French

J’ai bien compris que vous souhaitez [livrable précis] afin de [objectif métier]. Avant de confirmer un prix fixe et un délai, j’aimerais préciser [une ou deux questions de périmètre].

Je propose de procéder par étapes :
1. Valider les livrables, les éléments fournis et les critères de réception.
2. Présenter une première version pour retour.
3. Appliquer les modifications convenues et tester le résultat.
4. Livrer les fichiers avec une courte note de prise en main.

Je confirmerai le prix et le délai après clarification du périmètre et ne m’engagerai que sur les fonctionnalités que je peux réellement réaliser et vérifier.

Pourriez-vous partager [élément ou précision indispensable] ?

Cordialement

## Safety and conversion checklist

- Confirm the platform's legal eligibility, fees, payout options, and dispute policy.
- Never pay a client to access work, accept a fake cheque, or move into suspicious payment/crypto flows.
- Keep first projects small and milestone-based; confirm payment arrangements in writing.
- Use genuine work samples only. If no client portfolio exists, create clearly labelled sample projects instead of claiming client results.
- Do not send proposals until the account owner reviews the profile and confirms the actual service capability, price, and timeline.
