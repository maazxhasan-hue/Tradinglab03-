## Qualification gates before any proposal

1. Open the original listing and confirm it is still accepting offers.
2. Confirm remote/international eligibility and any platform-specific application rules.
3. Extract exact deliverables, deadline, required stack, content/assets, revisions, hosting, maintenance, and acceptance criteria.
4. Confirm budget/payment process and milestone terms on-platform; never pay a buyer to unlock work or accept off-platform payment pressure.
5. Check whether the required work can genuinely be delivered and tested; don't claim prior results, portfolio work, or capabilities that have not been verified.
6. Only then draft a customized proposal for human approval. No messages, bids, or submissions were sent during this review.
