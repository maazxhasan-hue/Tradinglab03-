# TradingLab03 — Client Acquisition Launch Pack

**Purpose:** win the first real paid digital-service project using free channels and careful, owner-approved outreach.

**Guardrails:** no paid subscriptions or ads, no Freelancer.com, no auto-bidding, no automated outbound messages, no fake portfolio claims, and no invented prices or deadlines. The owner approves every message and commercial offer before sending. Count revenue only after verified payment.

## 1. The offer to lead with

Start with one small, easy-to-understand service rather than advertising everything at once.

**Primary offer — Mobile-first one-page business website**
- A responsive landing page for a small business.
- Clear services, contact/enquiry path, and mobile-friendly layout.
- Basic page titles/meta description and working-link checks.
- Client-approved copy and supplied brand assets.
- Scope, price, timeline, revisions, ownership, and handover agreed in writing before work begins.

**Other services when the buyer's need fits**
- Website cleanup: fix specifically agreed issues.
- Short-form content starter: hooks, scripts, captions, and editing instructions.
- Basic workflow automation: only after data, permissions, error handling, and acceptance tests are agreed.

Do not promise sales, rankings, views, revenue, or investment returns. Do not start implementation until the client approves the scope and price.

## 4. Personalised outreach templates

Only use a channel where the recipient/platform permits the message. Verify the original source, buyer intent, and any contact rules. Send one relevant note; do not spam or repeatedly follow up after a refusal.

### Response to a public project request
Hello — I saw your request for [specific deliverable] on [source]. TradingLab03 helps with [matching service]. Based on your post, a possible first version would include [one or two relevant deliverables]. I would want to confirm [important unknown] before quoting a price or timeline. If you're still looking, I can send a short scope for review. Portfolio: https://maazxhasan-hue.github.io/TradingLab03/

### Warm introduction
Hi [name] — I'm building TradingLab03, a small digital-services studio focused on mobile-first business websites, landing pages, website cleanup, and short-form content. If you or someone you know has a specific project coming up, I'd be happy to review a short brief and tell you honestly whether it fits. No pressure either way.

### Follow-up after an invited discussion
Thanks for the details. Before I quote, could you confirm: (1) the main goal, (2) required pages/features, (3) what content/assets you already have, (4) your target deadline, and (5) your budget range? I'll return a version-one scope with inclusions, exclusions, acceptance checks, and any future features listed separately.

**Do not send these templates automatically.** Personalise them with a real observed need and get owner approval first.

## 5. Lead qualification checklist

Keep a lead only if:
- [ ] The original source is reachable and current.
- [ ] The person is seeking to buy a defined service.
- [ ] A concrete need matches an existing capability.
- [ ] Budget, deadline, language, and platform/payment rules are known or marked unknown.
- [ ] No upfront fee, suspicious deposit, unpaid test, or unrelated crypto request is involved.
- [ ] Evidence, source URL, date checked, and uncertainties are recorded.
- [ ] A relevant sample exists and is honestly labelled.
- [ ] The contact channel permits the response and the owner approves it.

Use statuses: New → Researching → Qualified → Needs clarification → Contact approved → Proposal sent → Won/Lost. Keep do-not-contact requests respected.

## 6. Seven-day operating rhythm

Each day, aim for quality over volume:
1. Review the latest opportunity-scout artifact and open original listings.
2. Qualify up to five promising leads; reject irrelevant, stale, risky, or fee-gated listings.
3. Prepare at most three genuinely personalised response drafts.
4. Owner reviews and manually sends only approved messages.
5. Publish one useful original content post or improve a portfolio sample.
6. Log replies, next actions, and explicit opt-outs.
7. Track qualified leads, replies, scope requests, proposals, wins, delivery quality, verified receipts, fees, and refunds.

If there are no qualified leads, improve the portfolio or content rather than manufacturing prospects.

## 7. Client-to-payment process

1. Confirm the buyer's goal and requirements.
2. Send a written version-one scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, dependencies, revisions, support boundary, price, timeline, payment milestones, and ownership terms.
3. Start only after written approval and the agreed initial payment/milestone, using a lawful payment method agreed with the client.
4. Build against the approved scope; put new ideas in a separate change request.
5. Test each acceptance criterion and share a review preview.
6. Fix in-scope defects, get written acceptance, and hand over agreed files/access securely.
7. Record payment only after checking the actual payment account; subtract fees and account for refunds.
