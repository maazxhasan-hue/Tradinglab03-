## Ready-to-review post 1 — website clarity

**Hook:** If a customer lands on your website, can they tell what you do in five seconds?

A useful small-business homepage should answer three questions quickly:
1. What do you offer?
2. Who is it for?
3. What should a visitor do next?

A clear message, readable mobile layout, proof you can genuinely stand behind, and a working contact route can make a website easier to use.

TradingLab03 is building practical website and landing-page concepts for small businesses. We confirm the brief and scope before quoting.

**CTA:** What is the one thing you wish your current website explained better?

## Ready-to-review post 2 — mobile checklist

**Hook:** Four quick checks for your business website on a phone.

- Is the main service clear without scrolling too far?
- Can a visitor tap the phone/email/contact action easily?
- Are headings and body text readable without zooming?
- Do the important links and forms actually work?

These are basic checks, not a promise of higher rankings or sales. If something fails, write down the exact page and behavior before planning a fix.

**CTA:** Save this checklist for your next website review.

## Ready-to-review post 3 — concept transparency

**Hook:** A portfolio concept should show the thinking, not pretend to be a client result.

Our Northline sample is a fictional small-business website concept. It demonstrates a responsive layout, a clear service structure, and a contact call to action. It is not a real client project and has no claimed business results.

That is the standard we want to keep: show the work, label the context, and never invent testimonials or metrics.

**CTA:** See the sample concept through the TradingLab03 portfolio page.

## Direct prospect research worksheet

For each business, record only publicly visible facts and the exact page URL:

- Business name and public website
- Public source URL and date checked
- Specific observable issue (e.g., broken navigation, no mobile contact action, unclear service list)
- Screenshot/notes if lawful and appropriate
- Potential small service that could address that exact issue
- Whether outreach is appropriate under platform rules and applicable law
- Draft message status: `not drafted` → `draft ready` → `human approved` → `sent by owner`
- Reply and next action, only after they occur

Do not harvest private personal data, scrape login-only areas, send bulk unsolicited messages, or automate comments/DMs. Do not infer a business has budget or interest from a website issue.

## Launch blockers

- Replace the portfolio's placeholder contact route with a real, owner-controlled contact method before promoting it.
- Confirm which services can actually be delivered and define a small starter scope.
