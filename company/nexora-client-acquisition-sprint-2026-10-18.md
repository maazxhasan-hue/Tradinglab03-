**Small-business website clarity sprint**
- Review one homepage on mobile and desktop.
- Deliver a short annotated issue list and a prioritized improvement plan.
- If the buyer approves, implement a narrowly scoped landing page or agreed fixes.
- Agree scope, access, acceptance checks, timeline, and price in writing before work starts.

## Organic visibility plan
1. Publish one useful website checklist or teardown each week, with a clear example and a non-pushy link to Nexora.
2. Post short, genuinely helpful tips: mobile contact checks, five-second homepage clarity, form testing, and basic accessibility.
3. Share only clearly labelled fictional concept work; never present a concept as a client project.
4. Join relevant Reddit/LinkedIn discussions only when community rules allow it. Answer the question first; avoid repetitive promotional comments and unsolicited bulk DMs.
5. Ask satisfied customers for honest reviews only after actual delivery; never fabricate reviews or buy engagement.
6. Add Google Search Console and a verified business profile only when the owner can complete account verification. Check indexing and search queries weekly.
7. Track qualified enquiries, replies, scoped proposals, contracts, and verified payments—not views alone.

## Posting draft — ready for owner review
**Hook:** Can a new visitor understand your business website in five seconds?

A strong homepage quickly answers:
- What do you offer?
- Who is it for?
- What should the visitor do next?

On mobile, check the headline, tap targets, contact path, and whether forms actually work. A polished page matters, but clarity and a working enquiry route matter too.

Nexora AI helps businesses plan practical website improvements and focused first releases. Every project starts with a clear scope—no inflated promises.

**CTA:** What is one thing you wish your website made easier for customers?
