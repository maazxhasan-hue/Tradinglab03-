## What the requester appears to need

- Batch conversion of question-paper PDFs with roughly 200–500+ multiple-choice questions per file.
- A fixed 24-column Excel structure, including question text, A–D options, answer/explanation/hint fields, and question/option image references.
- Gujarati and English text, numerals, symbols and mathematical expressions preserved as printed.
- Images and diagrams extracted and associated with the correct question or option.
- No AI rewriting, spelling correction, summarization or silent modification of source text.

## Proposed first milestone: feasibility sample

Before quoting a full system, ask the requester to share (through a mutually agreed safe channel):
1. One representative PDF, ideally containing both text-based and scanned pages if both occur in their corpus.
2. Their exact Excel template with the 24 column headers and a small example of an accepted output row.
3. A human-verified expected output for 10–20 representative questions, including several image-bearing questions/options.
4. Clarification of which fields are present in the source and which (for example, correct answer, explanation, hint or category) are supplied separately.
5. Approximate total PDF count, languages, page formats, deadline, budget range and whether the PDFs may be processed locally/offline.

Use redacted or non-sensitive sample material where possible. Do not request credentials or private data.

## Feasibility approach

1. Inspect the sample to determine whether text is digitally embedded, scanned, or mixed.
2. Extract embedded text and images deterministically where possible; use local OCR only for pages that require it.
3. Preserve raw extracted text separately from any normalized display values. Do not auto-correct or paraphrase.
4. Export image files with stable IDs and create an explicit mapping table from question/option to image filename.
5. Produce an Excel workbook matching the supplied headers exactly; leave source-absent fields blank rather than inventing answers or explanations.
6. Add a review report that flags uncertain OCR spans, missing images, duplicate IDs and ambiguous question/option associations.
7. Compare a human-verified sample against the output before estimating batch throughput.

## Acceptance criteria for the sample milestone

- Exact 24-column order and header names match the client's template.
- A reviewer can trace each exported row back to its PDF page and question.
- Source text is not silently rewritten; uncertain characters are flagged for review.
- Extracted images have stable filenames and an auditable question/option mapping.
- Fields not present in the source or separately supplied data remain blank and are listed as open questions.
- Known limitations and manual-review requirements are documented.

**No promise of 100% OCR accuracy:** exact preservation, Gujarati OCR quality, formula recognition and image association must be measured on the supplied sample. If a sample cannot meet the agreed checks with the available free/local tools, report that limitation before committing to the full build.
