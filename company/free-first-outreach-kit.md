# TradingLab03 — Free-First Outreach & Portfolio Kit

**Updated:** 2026-10-15  
**Purpose:** Turn qualified opportunities into respectful, specific conversations without subscriptions or mass outreach.  
**Status:** Draft materials only. Nothing in this file has been sent.

## Positioning

TradingLab03 offers small, clearly scoped digital deliverables. Start with a service you can actually complete, show a clearly labelled sample, and agree on scope before quoting.

### Offer A — One-page business landing page
**Best for:** A local service business that needs a clear explanation of services and a simple enquiry path.

**Deliverables**
- Responsive single-page layout
- Services, process, FAQ, and contact sections using client-approved content
- Basic title/description and semantic structure
- Mobile/desktop and link checks
- Source files and a short handover note

**Not included unless agreed:** domain/hosting fees, paid plugins, copywriting beyond the agreed scope, booking/payment integrations, ongoing maintenance.

### Offer B — Website cleanup
**Best for:** An existing site with a specific, reproducible layout or content issue.

**Deliverables**
- Confirmed issue list and baseline
- Agreed fixes only
- Before/after checks for the named pages
- Handover notes

**Not included:** guaranteed SEO rankings, broad redesign, untested production changes, or third-party costs.

### Offer C — Short-form content starter
**Best for:** A small brand that can supply its footage and brand assets.

**Deliverables**
- A small batch of hooks, scripts, captions, and edit instructions
- Platform-format checklist
- One agreed revision round

**Not included:** guaranteed views, follower growth, filming, licensed music, paid stock, or posting unless separately agreed.

## Public prospect qualification checklist

Only prepare an individual note after manually confirming:
- [ ] The business and website are real and relevant.
- [ ] There is a specific, observable improvement worth asking about.
- [ ] The observation is factual and not framed as a proven defect without evidence.
- [ ] The service is within current delivery capability.
- [ ] The contact channel and its rules allow the proposed message.
- [ ] The message is one-to-one, brief, respectful, and easy to decline.
- [ ] No sensitive personal data, scraped private data, or guessed private email is used.
- [ ] The note has human approval before it is sent.

## Template 1 — First contact about a website

**Subject:** A small website improvement idea for [Business]

Hello [Business/team],

I came across [public website/page] while looking at [relevant context]. One possible improvement to explore is [specific observation visible on the public page]. I may be missing context, so I wanted to ask rather than assume it is a problem.

TradingLab03 helps with small, clearly scoped website improvements. If this is relevant, I can outline a small first step and the expected deliverables before any commitment.

Would you be open to hearing the idea? No worries if it is not a priority.

Regards,  
TradingLab03

## Template 2 — Reply to a public request for help

Hello [name],

I saw your public request about [specific requirement] on [original post link]. I can help with [only the part you can genuinely deliver]. A sensible first step would be to confirm [one or two key scope questions], then agree the deliverables and acceptance checks.

My proposed scope would exclude [important exclusions] unless we agree otherwise. I have not assumed a price or deadline before understanding the details.

Would you like me to outline that scope?

Regards,  
TradingLab03

## Template 3 — Follow-up (only if appropriate and permitted)

Hello [name],

Just following up once on my note about [specific project]. If it is not a fit or the timing is not right, no reply is needed and I will leave it here.

Regards,  
TradingLab03

Do not send follow-ups if the recipient declines, asks not to be contacted, or the platform/community rules prohibit them.

## Proposal outline

1. Client goal in their words.
2. Observed requirements and what still needs clarification.
3. Deliverables and acceptance checks.
4. Exclusions, dependencies, and client-supplied assets.
5. Timeline only after confirming capacity and scope.
6. Price, payment milestones, taxes, and revision terms only after the owner approves them.
7. Handover, support boundary, and change-request process.

## Tracking rules

Record only observable stages: `new`, `qualified`, `draft_ready`, `awaiting_approval`, `contacted`, `reply_received`, `scope_confirmed`, `contracted`, `in_progress`, `QA`, `delivered`, `invoiced`, `paid`, `closed`.

- Do not mark contacted until a message was actually sent.
- Do not mark contracted without an accepted agreement.
- Do not mark paid until a receipt or account record confirms payment.
- Track expenses and refunds before calculating profit.
- Never buy a subscription or send a message automatically.
