Harvey's pipeline

● approval-first
Based on the supplied 8-page Harvey setup guide and the official repository README.
1

Discover businesses

OpenStreetMap is the free default. Always estimate paid discovery costs first.

2

Profile public websites

Read websites, robots.txt and sitemaps; identify evidence such as booking gaps, stale blogs, ad pixels and technology. Designed to avoid model calls.
3

Confirm research signals and score leads

Harvey proposes signals; choose which to collect and build a cohort from fit and disqualifying criteria.

4

Write personalized email drafts

Drafts should reference real prospect facts and remain in the outbox.
5

Review every email

Approve, edit or reject each message in Harvey's dashboard. Nothing should send without your approval.

6

Read replies and maintain the pipeline

The heartbeat prioritizes replies, approved campaigns, campaign drafts, prospecting, then analysis. Default loop is about every 15 minutes.

Compliance: contact businesses honestly, identify the sender, include a clear opt-out, follow applicable laws (such as CAN-SPAM/GDPR), use a separate sending domain, and never buy email lists.
